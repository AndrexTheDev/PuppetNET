      Mouse

      • Click a node — make it active, inspect it, highlight its ties.
      • Double-click a node — expand its neighbourhood by the current depth.
      • Shift+click — add to the selection (multi-select for the table).
      • Alt+click — hide the node and its edges from the current view.
      • Click an edge — inspect confidence, weight, method and citations.
      • Right-click a node — quick actions: expand, isolate, path A/B, hide.
      • Drag background — pan · scroll — zoom · drag node — pin it (double-click background to unpin all).

      Where the numbers come from

      • betweenness, degree_spike, offshore_cluster_ratio, anomaly_score, cluster_id are written by graph_analytics.py --centrality (Brandes / GDS).
      • Edge weight is the source-trust × extraction-method score; confidence is the noisy-OR of every observation.
      • Citations are Document nodes reached through MENTIONS, or the doc_id/source_id recorded on the edge.
      Zero external requests: every asset is vendored in web/vendor/.
